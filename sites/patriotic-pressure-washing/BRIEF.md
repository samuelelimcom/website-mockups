# Patriotic Pressure Washing, LLC: concept brief

Cleveland, TN (Bradley County). Google: 4.6 from 10 reviews. Phone (423) 599-4141.

trade and flagged in the footer as examples to confirm with the owner.

## Who buys this, here
- Homeowners 35 to 70 in Cleveland and the small towns around it: brick ranches, vinyl-sided
  two-storeys in newer subdivisions off APD-40 and Paul Huff, and a lot of wooden back decks,
  front porches and privacy fences.
- Triggers: a graduation party, a family visit for the holidays, listing the house for sale,
  an HOA letter, or the spring moment when the deck turns out to be grey and slick.
- Fears: someone gouging their deck or blowing water behind siding; a no-show; an unclear
  price; strangers on the property. They want a local person who answers the phone.

## How they arrive
- From Google Maps on a phone, often standing in the yard looking at the problem.
- In 10 seconds they need: what is washed (deck, house, driveway), that it is local, the
  rating, and a big call button. The sticky call/quote dock does that.

## Competitors (from directory listings; their own sites could not be opened here)
- Magic Stick Power Washing: broad "one-stop" positioning, generic stock look.
- Covenant House Washing: wide service list (siding, gutters, decks, windows). Strong on range.
- T-MAC Pressure Washing: says low pressure on houses, high on concrete. Good idea, buried.
- Gap: nobody shows *the wood coming back*. Decks are the most emotional, visible job in this
  town and no local site makes the before/after felt. Most are blue-and-water clip art.

## Positioning and tone
- Line: "Every board, back to its colour." Supporting: the right pressure for each surface.
- Tone: plain-spoken, neighbourly, porch-step talk. Short sentences. No hype, no claims.
- Home story arc: (problem) the deck went grey and slick a summer at a time → (turn) washed
  with the grain, one board at a time → (result) the cedar colour is back and the porch is
  somewhere you want to sit again.

## Design
- Palette: porch-paper cream `#f3ede1` (warm, outdoor-readable, not clinical white), ink navy
  `#16233a` (trust, and it nods to the business name), brick red `#a8322a` for actions (the
  colour of the brick ranches around town; reads as "do this"), cedar `#b8743a` for the wood
  reveal. Red/navy are used like old hardware-store signage, not flags; no veteran claims.
- Type: Bitter (slab serif, one variable family). Sign-painted, sturdy, readable at arm's
  length on a phone in sun; distinct from the sans-serif look of every competitor.
- Layout: editorial ledger. Square corners, ruled lines, numbered rows, letterpress-offset
  buttons. Services shown as a ledger list rather than glossy cards.
- 3D concept: a corner of a real back porch. Deck boards with grain and gaps, a railing with
  balusters, and a brick step. The boards start silver-grey and weathered; a pressure wand
  works along the grain one board at a time and the warm cedar comes back. Visitors drag the
  wand along a board to clean it themselves (snaps to the board, like the real technique).
  Service pages: the same porch for decks, a siding-and-brick wall for house washing, and a
  concrete walk with brick edging for driveways.
