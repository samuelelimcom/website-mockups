# Pole Clean Ltd: concept brief

**Business:** Pole Clean Ltd, Stoke-on-Trent (UK). Window cleaning company (Companies House SIC 81221,
window cleaning services; incorporated April 2020). Google rating 5.0 from 159 reviews. No website found
by name or phone search on 2026-11-26. Everything below that is not in that line is about the trade
and the town in general, not a claim about this business.

## Who buys this, here
- Homeowners and tenants in the Potteries towns (Hanley, Burslem, Tunstall, Stoke, Fenton, Longton) and
  the suburbs around them: mostly Victorian and inter-war red-brick terraces and semis, plus newer estates.
  Many uPVC windows, porches and conservatories.
- Typical buyer: 35 to 70, busy or less able to get up a ladder, house-proud, often a couple or a retired
  household. Also landlords and small shops.
- Trigger: moving in, the old window cleaner retiring or vanishing, the low winter sun showing every smear,
  green streaks appearing on white uPVC, or getting the house ready for a visit or a sale.
- Fears: someone who turns up irregularly, ladders against the house and in the flower beds, smears and
  water spots afterwards, being stuck in a round they cannot cancel, cash-only awkwardness.

## How they arrive and what they need in 10 seconds
- From Google Maps on a phone, usually after asking a neighbour "who does your windows?"
- In 10 seconds: is this a real local window cleaner, do they do my sort of house, are they rated well,
  and how do I get a price without a long call. Big phone button, rating, a short form.

## Competitors (what we saw)
- Directory-led competitors (Checkatrade, Bark, MyBuilder, Yell listings for Stoke and Newcastle-under-Lyme):
  good at proof (ratings) but every listing looks the same, with no sense of the actual work.
- Small local sites such as Williams Exterior Shine and Hydro Shine Exterior Cleaning (Newcastle-under-Lyme):
  clear service lists and ST postcode coverage, but template layouts, stock photos and long text.
- Pure Transparent / Aquavan style "pure water" firms: explain reverse-osmosis water well, but in jargon.
- **Gap:** nobody shows *why* a water-fed pole gives a better finish in a way a homeowner gets instantly.
  A site that lets you scrub a grimy window yourself and watch it dry clear is memorable and shareable.

## Positioning, tone, story
- Positioning: "Clear windows, from the ground up." Pole work means no ladders against the house, and pure
  water means no smears.
- Tone: plain Staffordshire friendliness. Short sentences, no jargon, a bit of dry humour. British spelling.
- Home page arc:
  1. **Problem:** a winter-grimy window. "You notice it when the sun gets low."
  2. **Turn:** the brush comes up on a pole from the pavement. Scrub frames and glass, no ladder.
  3. **Rinse:** pure water rinses top to bottom and carries the dirt away.
  4. **Result:** it dries clear with nothing left behind to spot. The whole front of the house looks cared for.
  Then proof (Google rating), services, how it works, quote form.

## Design
- **Palette:** warm limestone paper `#f4efe6` (calm, domestic, not clinical), Staffordshire brick
  `#8e3b26` for actions (the colour of every terrace in the city), deep slate ink `#1c2530` for type
  (Welsh slate roofs, high contrast), pale glass blue `#d8ecf3` and clear sky `#2f7fa8` as the "clean" accent.
- **Type:** Schibsted Grotesk, one variable family. Sturdy, newspaper-honest and very legible on phones;
  feels local and plain-speaking rather than corporate.
- **Layout:** light, airy, generous white space (the product is clarity). Cards look like panes of glass:
  thin frame borders, rounded corners like uPVC sashes. Squared, solid buttons.
- **3D concept:** a slice of red-brick Potteries terrace: white uPVC casement window with a stone sill,
  brick lintel, white fascia and black gutter above. The glass is grimy. A telescopic water-fed pole with
  a brush rises from below; it scrubs, pure water sheets down, and the pane dries clear with a glint.
  Visitors scrub it themselves with mouse or finger. Service pages reuse the facade with the frames and
  sill (uPVC) or the fascia and gutter line dirty instead.
